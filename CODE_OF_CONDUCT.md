# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

In the interest of fostering an open, welcoming, and respectful community, contributors and maintainers of BOSC Community Library pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity or expression, level of experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, inclusive, academic, and collaborative environment.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

- Showing empathy and respect toward other people
- Giving and receiving constructive feedback gracefully
- Respecting different viewpoints and lived experiences
- Taking responsibility for mistakes and learning from them
- Focusing on what is best for the project and community
- Using welcoming and professional language

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- Harassment, intimidation, or discriminatory language
- Personal attacks, insults, or trolling
- Publishing private information without permission
- Repeatedly derailing discussions or reviews
- Unwelcome sexual attention or imagery
- Any conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional academic setting

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing standards of acceptable behavior. They may take fair and appropriate corrective action in response to behavior they deem inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.

Maintainers have the right and responsibility to remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and other contributions that are not aligned with this Code of Conduct.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all project spaces, including:

- GitHub issues and pull requests
- Project documentation and code review discussions
- Community discussions linked to the repository
- Any public or private interaction representing the project

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, report it to the project maintainers through GitHub communication channels as soon as possible. Include as much relevant detail as you can so the matter can be reviewed fairly.

All reports should be handled with care, respect, and reasonable confidentiality.

## Enforcement Guidelines

When reviewing reported incidents, maintainers may consider:

- The impact of the behavior
- Whether the conduct was repeated
- Whether community safety is at risk
- The severity of the situation

Possible responses include:

- A private warning
- A request to revise language or behavior
- Temporary suspension from project spaces
- Permanent removal from project participation
